Where I stand

Six priorities, one focus: a Larkspur that works for the people who live here.

My top priority is revitalizing Magnolia Avenue — too many storefronts have sat vacant while corporate landlords wait out the market. Everything else follows from the same principle: practical results over process.

Top Priority

Revitalizing Magnolia Avenue

Holding corporate landlords accountable for vacant storefronts and bringing our commercial corridor back to life with policy that rewards occupancy, not speculation.

Flood & Fire Resilience

Real infrastructure investment and preparedness — not just more studies — so our streets and homes can withstand the next flood, fire, or insurance shock.

Housing That Fits Larkspur

Supporting solutions that ease our housing shortage and affordability pressure while respecting the character of our neighborhoods.

Small Business Support

Cutting the red tape that makes it hard to open, run, and keep a small business alive on our commercial streets.

Strong Public Schools

As a father with kids moving through Larkspur schools, I know what strong local schools need from the city — and I'll fight to give it to them.

An Open City Hall

Residents deserve a council that communicates clearly and often — not one you have to chase down to find out what's happening.

About Gareth

27 years leading teams through hard problems. Now I want to put that to work for Larkspur.

“I've spent my career solving hard problems and delivering results under pressure. Larkspur needs council members who listen first and turn plans into action.”

— Gareth Walter

After nearly three decades building and leading technology teams — including a decade at Salesforce and, most recently, as an executive at Vertafore — I retired to give my full attention to this community. I co-founded Rock Marin to bring music education to kids across Marin County, and I bring that same hands-on, get-it-done approach to city government.

As a father raising my kids here, I've watched flooding, aging infrastructure, and empty storefronts test our small town. I'm not a career politician — I'm your neighbor, with the time and experience to get things done.
